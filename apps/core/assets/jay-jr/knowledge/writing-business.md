### Clarity Is the Offer

### Put the paywall where the value is
2026-05-31 · Business · https://jiaweing.com/blog/put-the-paywall-where-the-value-is

### Your startup doesn't need a moat

### The razor and blades model

You look at your receipt and realize the accessories cost more than the camera itself. This pattern has a name.
